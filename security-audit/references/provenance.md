Inspired by [Claire Vo’s original X post](https://x.com/clairevo/status/2113068918239854908), also available through an [indexed mirror](https://teapawt.org/clairevo/status/2113068918239854908). Direct X retrieval was blocked during development. The original covers forgotten signups, connected services and old keys, AI connectors, dependencies, and a commentable sheet. This skill is an independent adaptation, not an endorsed Claire Vo product or verbatim reproduction.

The earlier October 23, 2026 draft had the same five areas. This revision preserves the useful inventory intent while replacing broad collection, credential fragments, raw history searches, production-only dependency checks and automatic cloud export. It adds explicit scope, evidence states, coverage gaps, safe report formatting and separate remediation authorization.
